Broader interests

I believe many of the world’s biggest problems can be solved through intelligent humanoid robots and AI superintelligence. I want to help build systems that can reason through difficult problems and act intelligently in the physical world.

My interests span robot learning (VLAs, WAMs, RL) and improving the capabilities of large language models. In robotics, I’m interested in how humanoids can learn useful skills and generalize to unfamiliar tasks and environments. For LLMs, I’m interested in advancing their ability to reason, learn, and solve increasingly complex problems through post-training and RL. What draws me to both is the possibility of turning progress in AI into useful work at a much larger scale.
